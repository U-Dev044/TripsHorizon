How to handle an enquiry

  1. Open a reservation and contact the customer using the details provided.
  2. Confirm supplier availability, final price, inclusions and cancellation terms.
  3. Send the customer your official payment instructions through your approved business process.
  4. Update the booking status only after you have verified the payment.

Never ask customers to send card PINs, passwords or one-time banking codes. Do not mark a booking as paid based only on a screenshot.
